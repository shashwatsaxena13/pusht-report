Per-start browser: standard PushT actions, attention

One row per test start (64 fixed initial positions), sorted by difficulty, measured as the mean steps to success of the default RL policies (AR, DD and flow, seeds 1 to 3).

  • Each panel overlays up to 8 rollouts per seed. These are rollout indices 0 to 7, every outcome, colored by episode time (inferno colormap). Seed 1 is drawn most opaque and seed 3 faintest.
  • The right columns give three lines per family. The mean steps over seeds (bold), the steps per seed (seed 1 / 2 / 3) and the speed per seed in px/step, all over successful RL rollouts.
  • The last column gives BC success counts. These are the BC checkpoints RL starts from, over all seeds.
  • Click a column header to sort. replay opens the default-setup player at that start.
